About the author

David P. Hoffman

Senior staff optical engineer · 10x Genomics

I’m a scientist, engineer, and inventor (7 issued U.S. patents and 6 additional published applications) with a proven track record of academic research and complex hardware system development. My science has been featured in Cell, Science (on the cover twice!), and Nature. My imaging systems power 10x Genomics’ flagship spatial biology platforms: Xenium and Atera. In graduate school at Berkeley, I trained as a physical chemist and spectroscopist before diving into imaging and biology as a postdoc in Nobel laureate Eric Betzig’s group. Then I transitioned to industry. While I enjoy digging into the nuts and bolts of hardware, theory, and code, I always see my work in the context of the big picture and strive to do the most useful thing most efficiently. I’m goal focused, data driven, and led by curiosity.
