01 / ABOUT

At home between
theory and the workbench.

I’m a scientist and engineer with a background in physical chemistry, a love of Python, and a focus on robust, high-performance optical systems.

At Berkeley, I trained as a physical chemist and spectroscopist. At Janelia, I moved into imaging and biology in Eric Betzig’s group. My work in hardware and software development has been featured on the cover of Science.

I enjoy the nuts and bolts of instrumentation, theory, and code. But the goal is always the bigger picture: doing the most useful thing, as efficiently as possible.

Also interested in lasers, microscopy,
and my daughter’s Legos.
